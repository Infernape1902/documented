# Atomic Structure and Properties

## 1. Atomic Structure
Atoms consist of:
- Protons (+1, nucleus)
- Neutrons (0, nucleus)
- Electrons (-1, outside nucleus)

---

## 2. Atomic Number & Mass Number

- Atomic number (Z) = protons
- Mass number (A) = protons + neutrons

\[
A = Z + N
\]

Neutrons:
\[
N = A - Z
\]

---

## 3. Isotopes
Same element, different neutrons.

- Same chemical properties
- Different mass

---

## 4. Average Atomic Mass
Weighted average of isotopes:

\[
\sum (\text{fraction abundance} \times \text{isotope mass})
\]

---

## 5. Moles and Molar Mass ⭐ (ADDED)

### Mole
- 1 mole = \(6.022 \times 10^{23}\) particles (Avogadro number)

### Molar mass (M)
- Mass of 1 mole of substance (g/mol)

---

## 6. Avogadro’s Number

\[
N_A = 6.022 \times 10^{23}
\]

---

## 7. Mole Calculations

\[
n = \frac{m}{M}
\]

Where:
- n = moles
- m = mass (g)
- M = molar mass

---

## 8. Particle Calculations

\[
N = n \times N_A
\]

---

## 9. Key Idea
- Mole connects microscopic particles → macroscopic mass
